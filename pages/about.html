Our Origin Story

From a Weekend Project to a Craft Business

It started in 2010 when founder Daniel Merritt bought a beat-up 1965 Airstream Overlander for $1,500. With no renovation experience, he spent six months watching videos, reading manuals, and learning by doing. The result was stunning — and the RV community took notice.

What began as a passion project quickly turned into a business. Within three years, we had a full workshop, a team of craftsmen, and a waiting list. Today, we're proud to be one of America's most trusted Airstream renovation studios.
